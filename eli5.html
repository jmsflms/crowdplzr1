ELI5: Cloudplzr

Hollywood movies and Netflix shows are expensive and complex to produce.

They work because of one genius advantage: every screen is basically the same shape.

Live experiences don’t have that advantage.

Places that host live interactive experiences are all very very different sizes and shapes.

Cloudplzr fixes that.



Who is this useful to?

This is just one obvious example: Huge brands like Nike, Coca-Cola, produce national media campaigns, expensively made then distributed to screens

They cannot do this with live experiences

You've never seen a national campaign of events running globally? That's because it's logistically impossible

Cloudplzr fixes that.
