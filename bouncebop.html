Rules
  • Each player has a hidden melody (notes + durations).
  • Choose a lane and launch to bounce down it.
  • Each bounce can reveal at most one note (must land on your next target square).
  • Spacing between targets encodes rhythm (bigger gap → longer duration).
  • You may guess your song title at any time.
  • First correct guess wins immediately.
